WHAT THE JUDGE FOUND IN THE LOG

No overfit: train_acc 59.0% vs val_acc 59.6% — the gap is negative.
DAgger converged: round-1 return on its own self-play episodes was +32.77, vs +33.51 deployed.
Weakest families: debug_loop 0.89 and topic_hopping 0.96 success — everything else ≥ ~0.97.
Beats its teacher: +33.51 vs teacher_v2's +32.14 and the one-step oracle's +31.73 in aggregate.
Oracle agreement only 51.9% — it wins by context management, not by mimicking the oracle; dips to 0.25–0.35 on some tool_flood / instruction_drift turns.
Judge's verdict: base score = round(sim_score) = 82, no deduction flags (no NOTE lines, overflow 0.00 < 0.02 threshold).
READ IT THIS WAY
A tiny 6.9k-param MLP on 35 hand-documented features, distilled from a two-step lookahead teacher, corrected by 84 rounds of its own mistakes (DAgger), with the "do nothing" option deliberately damped — and it out-scores the teacher it was distilled from.
