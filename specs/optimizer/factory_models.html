HOW THE CHAMPION IS CROWNED — TWICE

02 / 03 · selection & confirmation

THE FOUR GATES

1ANCHORED SCORING
The Judge reads the sim log and scores 0–100, but must stay within ±3 of the deterministic sim_score — otherwise the answer is rejected and retried. Opinions can't drift from the machine.
2RUNNING BEST
After each iteration, Python compares: higher judge score wins; tie → higher sim_score. Iteration 3 (82 / sim 82.28) beat 5 (82 / 81.95) and 6 (82 / 81.89) on the tie-break. Every win copies the model to best_model.py.
3TIE SET AT HALT
Picking the max of 12 noisy scores rewards luck (winner's curse). So at halt, every version within 2 standard errors of the champion's sim_score becomes a candidate — here, 6 versions tied statistically.
4THE HIDDEN EXAM
Each candidate is retrained and replayed on 200 fresh scenarios (seeds 95000+) never used during selection. New data, same deterministic simulator. The result is written to confirmation.json.
WHY TWO CROWNS? The spec's answer to "which is best" must be the judge-score rule (reproducible by definition). The evidence answer comes from the hidden exam. The factory reports both — and tells you when they disagree.
